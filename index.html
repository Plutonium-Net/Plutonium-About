The platform

Everything in one place

Plutonium Network is a collection of modern web services designed to cover entertainment, browsing, productivity and communication from one clean interface.

Games

pluto://games

Play a hand-picked library of custom HTML5 games, use games hosted on other CDNs, or upload and play your own browser games.

Internet

pluto://internet

Browse through Ultraviolet and Scramjet proxy technologies with search servers across the United States, Europe and Asia. Virtual machine streaming also provides a browser-in-browser option that avoids common proxy page-breaking issues.

AI

pluto://ai

Chat with a selection of AI models, with voice-to-voice conversation support also in development.

Cloud Gaming

pluto://cloud

Stream popular titles such as GTA 5, Subnautica, Hollow Knight and Elden Ring directly from the cloud without downloading them.

Media Services

pluto://media

Stream movies, television series and anime through one media service built into the platform.

Virtual Machines

pluto://vms

Stream Chromium virtual machines for a browser-in-browser experience through a remote desktop session.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Plutonium Network free? ▾
Yes absolutely free, forever. Every feature, from games to cloud gaming to AI, is free to use.
Is an account required? ▾
No. You can use the services as a guest. Creating an account enables cloud synchronization for supported activity and preferences across devices.
Can I protect my privacy at school or work? ▾
That's exactly what the built-in proxy engine is for. With the panic key and clean new-tab page, Plutonium helps you keep your browsing your own business.
How do I get help or suggest features? ▾
Join our Discord and say hi the community and team are always around for feedback and support.
